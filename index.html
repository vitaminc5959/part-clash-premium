Part Crash

How to play

  • Swap neighbours. Swap two touching parts so 3 or more of the same part line up in a row or column. They clear, new parts drop in, and chains score extra.
  • Make specials. Line up 4 to get a Scan Bar that wipes a row or column when matched. Line up 5 to get a Quality Seal: swap it with any part to clear every part of that kind.
  • Beat the clock. Clear matches before you run out of moves or the 1:15 timer runs out. Swaps that match nothing slide back and cost no move.
  • Rank up. Your score sets your rank live, from Trainee to Zero-Defect Master, and the board's theme shifts with it. Chain moves quickly for a COMBO multiplier up to ×3. You can give up and keep your points.
